Information we collect

KinJo collects the information you and your kindergarten provide to operate the enrollment, attendance, and reporting service: account details, child enrollment information, daily attendance and activity records, and messages exchanged on the platform.

How we use it

Information is used solely to provide the kindergarten management service to the kindergarten, its staff, and the parents of enrolled children. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Data retention & security

Data is stored on access-controlled systems with role-based permissions, audit logging, and encrypted password storage. Access is limited to the roles that need it to perform their duties (parent, kindergarten manager/supervisor, or system administrator).

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through the Contact Us page.
