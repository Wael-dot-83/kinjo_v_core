## What It Does

The "Enhance Prompt" feature uses advanced AI to analyze and improve your prompts, making them clearer, more specific, and optimized for better results. Whether you're crafting a question, instruction, or creative request, our enhancement technology helps you get the most out of AI interactions.

## How to Use

1. **Enter your prompt** in the input field above
2. **Click the "Enhance Prompt" button** 
3. **See the transformation** instantly displayed below

Our AI examines your input and applies proven prompt engineering techniques to improve clarity, structure, and effectiveness while preserving your original intent.

- **Clarity improvements**: Removes ambiguity and makes your intent unmistakable
- **Better structure**: Organizes your prompt for optimal AI understanding
- **Added context**: Includes relevant details that improve response quality
- **Model optimization**: Tailors enhancements for specific AI capabilities
- **Time savings**: Get better results faster without prompt engineering expertise
